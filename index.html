How to play

Do what the pad says before the ring runs out. Every 8 hits the tempo goes up, the key changes and the window gets shorter. You have 3 lives, and every 20-hit streak earns one back.

Hitting it in the first third of the window scores FAST (+50). Your streak builds a multiplier up to ×8. Daily mode gives everyone the same command sequence each day.
